01 Heritage

A Brantford landmark,
one plate at a time.

61years of family dining

“Gather your family and friends, and feast on good times.”

Since 1961, The Sherwood has served Brantford from the corner of Colborne Street — home-made breakfasts before the morning rush, hot beef and perch for the lunch crowd, Olde-English buffets every weekend afternoon, and a banquet hall that has seen three generations of weddings, anniversaries, and retirement parties.

Kitschy by design, family-run by choice, and open 364 days a year because a landmark doesn't close for the week. The kitchen cooks without oil or salt for special dietary needs, keeps gluten-free and dairy-free meals on request, and pours a proper Friar Tuck at the bar.
